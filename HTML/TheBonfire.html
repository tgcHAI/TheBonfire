EHO — 004
The Wayward & The Hearth
Historical Record Author: Office Archive Status: Sealed

There are those who build walls and those who build fires. The wall keeps things out. The fire keeps things alive. The Eternal Hearthlight Office was not built to be a fortress. It was built to be the light you can see from the road when you have been walking too long in the dark and you are beginning to wonder whether you have the wrong road or no road at all.

The Backstreets do not care about you. That is the first thing to understand. They are not hostile in the way that a predator is hostile — they do not select targets, do not stalk, do not plan. They are simply indifferent, which is in some ways worse, because indifference does not tire and cannot be reasoned with and will be there every morning you wake up inside it.

The office is warm tonight. Everyone is fed. I checked twice. I will check again before I sleep, which will be late, which is fine. The fire does not go out on its own. Someone has to tend it. I do not mind. I just wish, sometimes, that someone would ask if I was tired.

They came in two directions. The ones who kept moving became the Wayward Travelers — named honestly, because they had somewhere once and the having of it is now the thing that follows them. The ones who stopped, who turned around and built something in the wreckage, became us. Neither choice was wrong. Both choices cost something.
